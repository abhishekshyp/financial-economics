The business03

What is a business?

A business is a legal entity

In India, a company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 becomes a separate legal person, distinct from the people who own it.

  • Owns things in its own name: property, bank accounts, contracts.
  • Can sue and be sued in its own name.
  • Perpetual succession: owners can change, the company continues.
  • Limited liability: a shareholder can lose at most what they invested.
  • Registered with the Registrar of Companies (MCA) and gets a CIN (Corporate Identity Number).
