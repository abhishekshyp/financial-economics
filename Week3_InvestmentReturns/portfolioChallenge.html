Debrief · What the game taught04

Reward is easy. Risk-adjusted reward is the game.

Lesson 01 · Diversification

Portfolio risk < average of the parts

Because σₚ = √(wᵀΣw) and correlations are below 1, combining imperfectly-correlated assets lowers risk without a matching drop in return. Your portfolio dot sat left of a naïve weighted average of the σ's — that gap is a free lunch, in Markowitz's phrase.

Lesson 02 · Reward-to-variability

Return ÷ risk ranks portfolios, not return alone

A 100% small-cap book earns 18% but scores ≈0.51; a bond-and-gold tilt earns less yet scores higher. The ratio is a Sharpe-style measure (here without subtracting the risk-free rate).

Lesson 03 · Correlation is hidden risk

Equities move together when it matters

Large, mid, small and international equity carry high pairwise correlation (≈0.6–0.9), so an all-equity book is far less diversified than it looks. In the crisis they fell in unison.

Lesson 04 · Tail events

Safe havens earn their keep in a crisis

Government bonds and gold rose while equities and REITs fell — the flight-to-safety that shows up as low/negative correlation in calm times and pays off in the tail.
